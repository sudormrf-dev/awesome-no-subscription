# Media & Entertainment

Video players, music players, media servers, and media management tools.

---

### [VLC](https://www.videolan.org/)
:unlock: Open source (free) | Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android
**Replaces:** Paid video players, codec packs
Plays virtually any media format without requiring additional codecs. Supports streaming, subtitle downloading, audio/video filters, and media conversion. The universal media player.

### [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org/)
:unlock: Open source (free, self-hosted) | Windows, macOS, Linux, Docker
**Replaces:** Plex Pass ($5/mo or $120 lifetime), Emby ($5/mo)
Self-hosted media server for movies, TV shows, music, and photos. Stream to any device with client apps for web, mobile, smart TVs, and streaming boxes. No premium tier -- all features are free.

### [Infuse](https://firecore.com/infuse)
:moneybag: $10 one-time (Pro via one-time IAP) | macOS, iOS, tvOS
**Replaces:** Plex client, paid media players
Beautiful media player that connects to network shares, Plex, Emby, and Jellyfin servers. Hardware-accelerated playback, Dolby Atmos, 4K HDR, and automatic metadata fetching. Plays virtually any format.

### [MusicBee](https://getmusicbee.com/)
:free: Free | Windows
**Replaces:** Spotify ($11/mo) for local music libraries
Feature-rich music manager and player for local libraries. Gapless playback, auto-tagging, library organization, and extensive customization with skins and plugins. Handles libraries of 100,000+ tracks.

### [Doppler](https://brushedtype.co/doppler/)
:moneybag: $10 one-time | macOS, iOS
**Replaces:** Spotify ($11/mo) for local music playback
Elegant local music player for Apple platforms. Supports FLAC, ALAC, MP3, AAC, and more. Clean interface with gapless playback, EQ, and music library management. Designed for people who own their music.

### [mpv](https://mpv.io/)
:unlock: Open source (free) | Windows, macOS, Linux
**Replaces:** Any paid video player
Minimalist, high-quality media player. GPU-accelerated video output, extensive format support, scripting with Lua and JavaScript, and precise seeking. Preferred by power users for its performance and configurability.

### [PotPlayer](https://potplayer.daum.net/)
:free: Free | Windows
**Replaces:** Paid video players, codec packs
Feature-rich video player with hardware acceleration, 3D support, subtitle management, and extensive codec support. Lightweight with a customizable interface and advanced playback options.

### [foobar2000](https://www.foobar2000.org/)
:free: Free | Windows, macOS, iOS, Android
**Replaces:** Spotify ($11/mo) for local music playback
Advanced audio player with support for nearly every audio format. Fully customizable interface, gapless playback, ReplayGain, and extensive plugin support. Extremely lightweight and fast.

### [Navidrome](https://www.navidrome.org/)
:unlock: Open source (free, self-hosted) | Windows, macOS, Linux, Docker
**Replaces:** Spotify ($11/mo) with self-hosted streaming
Self-hosted music server compatible with Subsonic API clients. Stream your music library from anywhere. Supports multiple users, transcoding, playlists, and smart playlists. Minimal resource usage.

### [MusicBrainz Picard](https://picard.musicbrainz.org/)
:unlock: Open source (free) | Windows, macOS, Linux
**Replaces:** Paid music taggers and identification tools
Automatic music tagger using the MusicBrainz database. Identifies tracks by audio fingerprint, fixes tags and filenames, downloads album art, and organizes music libraries. Supports plugins for extended functionality.

### [Doppelgone](https://doppelgone.app)
:moneybag: $9.99 one-time (free for unlimited scans and 200 clean-ups) | Chrome (Google Photos, iCloud Photos), Windows, macOS, iOS, Android
**Replaces:** Buying more cloud storage to hold duplicate photos (Google One 100 GB, $1.99/mo)
Finds exact duplicates, lookalikes and burst shots in a photo library, keeps the best shot of each set and says why. You approve every removal, and removed photos wait 30 days in the service's own trash. Photos are analysed on your own device. One payment covers up to 3 libraries.
